Booking Controls

Only an admin can lock the schedule or turn priority booking on and off.

When locked, nobody (including admins and directors) can book or cancel anything until it is unlocked again.

When on, teams can only book once every older grade has finished: 6th grade first, then 5th, and so on down to Kindergarten, across both programs (all 6th grade teams finish before any 5th grade team unlocks). Each team’s coach or director taps Finished booking on Book once their team is done for the season. Reset Finished Teams starts the ladder over and clears any overrides.

Danger Zone

Clear by Team

Clear by Field

Deletes every booking at the site, on all of its fields. This cannot be undone.
