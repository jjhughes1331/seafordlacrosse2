Support

Seaford Lacrosse field scheduling

Seaford Lacrosse is the field-booking app for our coaches and directors. Accounts are invite only: your director creates yours, and you get an email with a link or a password.

Get help

Email seafordlacrosseinc@gmail.com. We usually answer within a day. Please include your name and your team.

Common questions

I can't sign in

Use the email address your director invited. Tap Forgot your password? on the sign-in screen to set a new one. If no email arrives, check your spam folder, then email us and we will set a password for you.

I don't have an account

Ask your team's director to invite you. There is no public sign-up.

I can't book yet

Teams book in grade order, oldest first. Your grade opens once the grades above yours have finished, or after their three days run out. The app tells you when.

A practice I booked is gone

A director or administrator can cancel any booking. Check the Activity list under Admin, or ask your director.

Delete my account

Open the menu (☰) and choose Delete my account. It is removed straight away. Practices your team already booked stay on the schedule.
